# Security Policy

This policy covers every public repository in the [decimal-labs](https://github.com/decimal-labs)
organization, and the services at `api.decimal.ai`, `app.decimal.ai`, and `docs.decimal.ai`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

Two ways to reach us, either is fine:

- **GitHub private vulnerability reporting** — on any of our public repositories, use
  **Security → Report a vulnerability**. This opens a private advisory that only maintainers
  can see, and keeps the whole exchange attached to the repository.
- **Email** — [hello@decimal.ai](mailto:hello@decimal.ai). We do not publish a PGP key. If you
  would rather not put the details in cleartext email, use the GitHub channel above — the advisory
  is visible only to you and the maintainers.

Useful things to include, to the extent you have them: what you found, the steps to reproduce it,
which version or endpoint you hit, and what an attacker could actually do with it. A rough
proof-of-concept is worth more than a careful description. If you only have a hunch, send the
hunch — we would rather triage a false alarm than miss a real one.

## What happens next

We are a small team, so we would rather tell you what we actually do than publish a service level
we cannot hold to:

- We acknowledge reports as soon as we have read them, and we will tell you plainly if triage is
  going to take a while.
- We will let you know whether we consider the report in scope, and what we plan to do about it.
- We follow [coordinated disclosure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_vulnerability_disclosure).
  We will agree a disclosure timeline with you rather than impose one, and we will not ask you to
  stay quiet indefinitely.
- We are happy to credit you in the advisory and the release notes. Tell us how you would like to
  be named, or say that you would rather not be.

We do not currently run a paid bug bounty. That is a resourcing decision, not a judgment about the
value of your work.

## Scope

**In scope** — anything in a public `decimal-labs` repository, and the production services listed
at the top of this file. That includes the published packages: `decimalai`, `agentversion`,
`skillevaluation`, and `decimalai-mcp` on PyPI, and the `regression-check` GitHub Action.

**Out of scope** — findings that require a compromised end-user device or account; social
engineering of our team or our users; volumetric denial of service; reports generated by a scanner
with no demonstrated impact; and missing hardening headers or best-practice deviations with no
exploitable consequence. Please also do not test against other people's data — if you need an
account to demonstrate something, tell us and we will help you set one up.

## Safe harbour

If you make a good-faith effort to follow this policy, we will not pursue or support legal action
against you for your research. Good faith means: you avoid privacy violations and service
degradation, you only interact with accounts you own or have permission to test, and you give us a
reasonable opportunity to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Something else

If you are not sure whether what you found is a security issue, email
[hello@decimal.ai](mailto:hello@decimal.ai) and ask. That is always the right call.
